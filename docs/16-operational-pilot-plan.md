# Operational Pilot Plan

**Version:** Phase III · 2026-05-17  
**Status:** 24-month internal plan for Northern Portugal pilot

---

## Purpose

This plan converts the earlier first-year pilot concept into a 24-month operating sequence. It prioritizes governance, consent and archive readiness before fieldwork, student activity or app development.

Public scope remains a small Northern Portugal pilot. No public claim should imply that practitioners, stewards or partners are confirmed until records exist.

---

## Pilot boundaries

| Boundary | Decision |
|----------|----------|
| Geography | Northern Portugal only |
| Sub-regions for research | Douro, Minho, Serra da Estrela, Tras-os-Montes |
| First proof target | 3 documented practitioner relationships |
| Student activity | Deferred until practitioner agreements and safety policies exist |
| App build | Prohibited before governance, consent and archive architecture |
| Atlas expansion | Internal only; no global corridor claims |

---

## Workstreams

| Workstream | Owner | Output |
|------------|-------|--------|
| Governance | Founder + advisor candidates | Entity path, advisor board, decision log |
| Consent | Founder + governance advisor | Consent workflow and forms |
| Archive | Archive steward | Metadata schema, access tiers, storage policy |
| Fieldwork | Founder + local coordinator | Practitioner conversations and documentation |
| Partnerships | Founder | Candidate list and outreach notes |
| Economics | Founder + legal / finance advisor | Compensation and funding model |
| Risk | Governance steward | Risk register and monthly review |

## Fieldwork cadence

| Stage | Cadence | Rule |
|-------|---------|------|
| Desk research | Weekly during months 1-6 | Internal only |
| Advisor review | Monthly during setup; quarterly after | Required before sensitive publication |
| Practitioner outreach | 1-2 active conversations at a time | Avoid volume pressure |
| Field visits | No more than one primary visit per month | Prepare consent and compensation first |
| Archive audit | Quarterly | No public record without linked consent |
| Risk review | Monthly | Update owners and stop criteria |

## Staffing assumptions

| Role | Minimum need | Notes |
|------|--------------|-------|
| Founder / project lead | 0.5-1.0 FTE during active fieldwork | Owns sequencing and documentation |
| Local coordinator | Part-time from months 5-18 | Helps with language, logistics and trust |
| Archive steward | Part-time from month 4 | Owns metadata and access control |
| Legal / governance advisor | Advisory | Reviews agreements and entity path |
| Documentation support | Contracted per session | Photography, audio or transcription only with consent |

---

## Partner targets

Partner outreach should prioritize categories, not public name-dropping:

- local craft schools or workshops;
- rural development organizations;
- cultural heritage groups;
- architecture or design programs;
- ecological restoration or land stewardship groups;
- municipal or regional cultural offices.

Every partner record should distinguish candidate, contacted, reviewing, active and declined.

---

## Success criteria

The 24-month pilot succeeds if:

- at least 3 practitioner relationships are documented with consent;
- governance review exists outside founder judgment;
- archive records have access tiers and withdrawal rules;
- practitioners report fair treatment and clear benefit;
- at least one compensation model is tested;
- at least one local partner or advisor relationship is verified;
- no unresolved consent, safety or representation harms remain open.

The pilot should pause if governance, consent or archive controls cannot be maintained.

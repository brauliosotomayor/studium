# Stewardship Operating Model

**Version:** Phase III · 2026-05-17  
**Status:** Internal operating model before pilot fieldwork

---

## Purpose

This document turns stewardship from a principle into operational authority. It defines who can review, pause, veto, escalate and audit Studium activity.

Stewardship is not a brand claim. It is a working governance function that must be documented before fieldwork, student recruitment or archive publication.

---

## Operating principle

Stewardship controls access, pace and representation. Growth is allowed only when steward capacity, practitioner consent and documentation review can keep up.

---

## Steward roles

| Role | Scope | Authority | Minimum evidence |
|------|-------|-----------|------------------|
| Practitioner steward | Own practice, workshop, teaching space and personal story | Approve, restrict or withdraw participation | Signed participation and media terms |
| Community steward | Community context, shared practice, local sensitivities | Review framing, access and reciprocity | Role note and review record |
| Land steward | Field site, ecological carrying capacity, seasonal access | Set seasonal limits and site restrictions | Site protocol |
| Archive steward | Documentation access, metadata, withdrawal and review cycles | Change access tiers and audit consent records | Archive access log |
| Governance steward | Policy compliance, conflicts, advisor escalation | Pause publication or activity pending review | Governance decision log |

One person may hold more than one steward role only if the conflict is documented and accepted.

---

## Authority model

| Activity | Required steward authority |
|----------|----------------------------|
| Add candidate to internal list | Founder review; no steward required |
| Contact practitioner | Founder review; local context check recommended |
| Conduct interview | Practitioner consent required |
| Photograph workshop or field site | Practitioner or land steward approval required |
| Publish profile | Practitioner approval plus steward review for Medium / High sensitivity |
| Host student visit | Practitioner approval, capacity limit and safety protocol |
| Create archive record | Consent record plus access tier |
| Open public applications | Governance steward confirms legal, consent and compensation readiness |

---

## Veto and pause rights

Stewards may pause or veto:

- publication of a practitioner profile;
- publication of images, video, audio, transcripts or field notes;
- exact location display;
- student access to a workshop, field site or community setting;
- archive access changes;
- expansion into a new geography or sensitive practice category.

A veto does not require public explanation. Internally, the decision should record the steward role, date, affected material and next review date.

---

## Review cycles

| Cycle | Frequency | Owner | Output |
|-------|-----------|-------|--------|
| Consent record audit | Before every publication and every 6 months | Archive steward | Consent status log |
| Practitioner relationship review | Every 6 months during pilot | Founder + practitioner steward | Continue / revise / pause |
| Fieldwork ethics review | Before each field trip | Governance steward | Fieldwork checklist |
| Archive access review | Quarterly | Archive steward | Access tier changes |
| Risk register review | Monthly during active pilot | Governance steward | Mitigation updates |
| Annual governance review | Every 12 months | Advisory board | Continue / pivot / pause recommendation |

---

## Escalation path

```text
Issue reported
    |
    v
Immediate restriction if harm or consent risk exists
    |
    v
Founder records issue and notifies relevant steward
    |
    v
Steward review within defined SLA
    |
    v
Advisor escalation if unresolved or high-risk
    |
    v
Decision logged: continue, restrict, repair, pause or withdraw
```

---

## Stewardship SLAs

| Sensitivity | Standard review time | Default if unresolved |
|-------------|----------------------|-----------------------|
| Low | 10 business days | Keep internal until approved |
| Medium | 14 business days | Restricted access |
| High | 30 business days | Restricted access; no student access |
| Active harm report | 5 business days for first response | Immediate pause |

Human review pace is a safeguard, not an inefficiency.

---

## Accountability records

Every stewardship decision should produce a record with:

- decision ID;
- date;
- material or activity affected;
- steward role and reviewer name when publishable;
- consent status;
- access tier before and after decision;
- reason category;
- next review date;
- link to related risk register item.

Do not store sensitive reasoning in public files.

---

## Operating boundaries

Studium should not:

- treat public interest as consent;
- replace community or practitioner review with internal founder judgment;
- rank sensitive practices by popularity;
- use documentation volume as the main success metric;
- use app analytics to override human review;
- frame stewards as symbolic advisors without actual decision rights.

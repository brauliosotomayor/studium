# Apprenticeship Framework

**Version:** Phase III · 2026-05-17  
**Status:** Operational draft before student recruitment

---

## Purpose

This framework defines allowable study formats, screening, capacity limits, practitioner compensation and community obligations for the Northern Portugal pilot.

No public recruitment should begin until governance, consent, compensation, insurance and field safety policies are approved.

---

## Program formats

| Format | Duration | Group size | Purpose | Status |
|--------|----------|------------|---------|--------|
| Study visit | 2-4 hours | 1-4 | Relationship introduction, observation and context | Pilot only |
| Field documentation session | Half day to 2 days | 1-3 | Interview, photography, archive preparation | Requires consent |
| Workshop immersion | 2-5 days | 3-6 | Hands-on learning with defined scope | After practitioner agreement |
| Seasonal residency | 1-4 weeks | 1-3 | Immersive practice, farm or workshop rhythm | Defer until pilot proves capacity |
| Apprenticeship | 1-6 months | 1-2 | High-engagement transmission relationship | Future only |

The first 24 months should emphasize study visits, documentation sessions and limited workshop immersions.

---

## Student screening

| Criterion | Required evidence | Decline signal |
|-----------|-------------------|----------------|
| Intent | Written statement explaining why this practice and place | Casual tourism or content-first motive |
| Capacity | Ability to complete full duration | Unclear availability |
| Respect for limits | Agreement to media, access and safety rules | Pushback on consent restrictions |
| Preparation | Relevant craft, field, ecology or study background when needed | No preparation for sensitive context |
| Contribution | Clear way to support documentation, labor, translation or learning community | Pure consumption framing |
| Conduct | References or interview for longer formats | Prior misconduct or unwillingness to follow protocol |

---

## Capacity limits

| Activity | Default cap | Rationale |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Practitioner interview | 1 interviewer + 1 documentarian | Reduce burden |
| Workshop immersion | 3-6 students | Support hands-on teaching |
| Field site visit | Set by land steward | Protect site and seasonal work |
| High-sensitivity practice | No student access by default | Require steward review |
| Repeat visits | Max 2 per practitioner in first year | Avoid overuse before review |

Capacity is a stewardship decision, not a demand target.

---

## Practitioner compensation

Compensation should be explicit before any visit. The pilot should budget for:

- preparation time;
- teaching or hosting time;
- documentation review time;
- translation or local coordination;
- materials used during teaching;
- travel reimbursement when needed;
- cancellation protection.

Avoid asking practitioners to participate for exposure, archive value or future platform upside.

---

## Compensation models to test

| Model | Best use | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| Day rate | Interviews, study visits, documentation sessions | Simple and respectful |
| Per-student fee | Workshops after capacity is known | Must include minimum guarantee |
| Residency stipend | Longer stays | Defer until legal and housing questions are solved |
| Materials fee | Craft inputs consumed by students | Separate from teaching compensation |
| Documentation review fee | Transcript, captions, media approval | Required when review time is significant |

---

## Community obligations

Every pilot activity should define:

- who benefits locally;
- who bears time, risk and disruption;
- what is returned to the practitioner or community;
- how documentation is shared back;
- how students are briefed on local conduct;
- what happens if the activity is paused or withdrawn.

Reciprocity can include payment, archive copies, translation, documentation support, student labor, local partner support or other practitioner-approved benefit.

---

## Practitioner agreement topics

Before any paid or public-facing activity, document:

1. teaching scope;
2. group size;
3. schedule and cancellation policy;
4. compensation and payment timing;
5. documentation permissions;
6. media restrictions;
7. student conduct rules;
8. safety and liability boundaries;
9. withdrawal and pause rights;
10. review date.

This framework is not a legal contract. It is the operating checklist for drafting one.

---

## Student agreement topics

Students should agree to:

- learning purpose and conduct rules;
- no recording unless approved;
- no public posting without written approval;
- safety instructions;
- no independent commercial reuse of teachings;
- respect for cancellation and seasonal limits;
- consent to removal if rules are violated.

---

## Pilot success criteria

The apprenticeship model is ready to continue only if:

- practitioners report that participation was worth the time and fairly compensated;
- documentation review is completed without unresolved consent issues;
- students complete the agreed duration and follow media rules;
- community or steward concerns are addressed;
- no safety, legal or representation issues remain unresolved;
- the next cohort would not increase burden beyond capacity.

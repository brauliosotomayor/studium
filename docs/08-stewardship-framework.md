# Stewardship & Sensitivity Framework

**Version:** Phase II · 2026-05-17  
**Status:** Superseded historical framework  
**Visibility:** Internal historical reference  
**Canonical replacements:** `11-governance-blueprint.md`, `12-stewardship-operating-model.md`, `13-consent-and-documentation-ethics.md`

This document records the Phase II ethics model. It no longer governs current operations where it conflicts with Phase III governance, stewardship authority or consent workflows.

---

## Principle

**Stewardship before scale.** The Steward role gives practitioners and communities authority over access, documentation and growth. This framework gates expansion.

---

## Sensitivity tiers

| Tier | Definition | Default gating |
|------|------------|----------------|
| **Low** | Established craft economies; low ritual restriction; public workshop norms | Standard application + master approval |
| **Medium** | Cultural context required; limited capacity; some media limits | Steward review within 14 days |
| **High** | Indigenous, ceremonial, ecologically fragile, or politically vulnerable lineages | Steward council + elder consent; restricted media |

Dataset field: `Sensitivity` in `studium_atlas_master.csv`.

---

## Permissions model

| Actor | May | Boundaries |
|-------|-----|---------|
| **Student** | Apply with stated intent; participate after acceptance | Application-based access; recording and commercial use require consent |
| **Master** | Define teaching scope, group size, duration | Listing, volume and participation remain consent-based |
| **Steward** | Gate access, set cultural guidelines, pause listings | Steward role centers protection, consent and reciprocity |
| **Studium (platform)** | Facilitate connection, archive with license | Own indigenous knowledge; algorithmically rank “popular” experiences |

---

## Representation rules

1. **Name and lineage** — Only publish what master and steward approve.  
2. **Indigenous nodes** (`Indigenous Y/N = Y`) — Require steward partner and context-led representation.  
3. **Ecological nodes** (`Ecological Y/N = Y`) — Disclose seasonal limits and land impact.  
4. **Ritual / ceremonial craft** — Use careful educational copy and protected location visibility when safety requires obscurity.  
5. **Photography** — Default off for High sensitivity; steward signs media agreement.

---

## Onboarding ethics (masters)

| Step | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| 1. Nomination | Peer, steward, or founder referral through consent-based channels |
| 2. Conversation | In-person or live video; assess teaching willingness |
| 3. Lineage documentation | Years of practice, teachers, place and transmission context |
| 4. Steward pairing | Every High/Medium indigenous or ritual node |
| 5. Pilot listing | One experience type first; expand after documented completion |
| 6. Review at 12 months | Continuation, pause, or elder-led removal |

## Stewardship review process

```
Application submitted
        │
        ▼
┌───────────────────┐
│ Master pre-screen │─── decline → feedback, good standing
└─────────┬─────────┘
          ▼
┌───────────────────┐
│ Steward review    │─── hold → questions, cultural fit
└─────────┬─────────┘
          ▼
┌───────────────────┐
│ Acceptance        │─── participation + documentation protocol
└───────────────────┘
```

**SLA:** Medium 14 days; High 30 days (human pace intentional).

---

## Anti-extraction rules

| Prohibited | Allowed |
|------------|---------|
| “Top 10 experiences” listicles | Curated corridor essays by scholars/stewards |
| Influencer bulk participation | Small cohort apprenticeships |
| Scraping master content for AI training | Archive with signed license |
| Repackaging teachings independently | Master-led digital supplements with consent |
| Venture-scale growth KPIs on node count | Node count gated by steward capacity |

---

## Media guidelines

| Sensitivity | Photo | Video | Student-generated content |
|-------------|-------|-------|---------------------------|
| Low | With consent form | Optional | CC-BY with master approval |
| Medium | Steward-approved shot list | Limited | Review before publish |
| High | Steward-only or none | Rare, elder approval | Archive internal by default |

**Archive purpose:** Preservation, education, consent and context.

---

## Application gating (all nodes)

Required fields (future app; enforce in ops now):

1. **Why this place, why now** (500 words min for High)  
2. **Prior craft or land experience**  
3. **Willingness to complete full apprenticeship duration**  
4. **Agreement to stewardship code**  
5. **Media consent tier selected**

**Auto-reject signals:** Tourism language; “content creation” primary motive; refusal of steward contact for High nodes.

---

## Priority populations

| Population | Rule |
|------------|------|
| **Indigenous knowledge holders** | Steward nation or community organization as co-gate |
| **Ecological systems** | Land carrying capacity cap per season |
| **Ceremonial traditions** | Steward-approved public presentation and pricing visibility |
| **Vulnerable lineages** | Fewer than N practitioners globally → High by default |
| **Women-only / initiation traditions** | Gender and initiation rules respected in listing visibility |

---

## Governance escalation

| Event | Action |
|-------|--------|
| Cultural harm report | Immediate listing pause |
| Master withdrawal | Node dimmed; archive preserved per license |
| Steward dispute | Founder council review |
| Media violation | Account suspension; content takedown |

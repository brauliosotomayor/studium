Purpose

This document defines the consent workflow for interviews, photography, field notes, archive records and future student participation. It is FPIC-aligned: free, prior, informed and consent-based, with withdrawal and restriction rights.

Consent is a process, not a signature. Documentation should remain restricted unless the relevant practitioner, steward or community review has approved a specific use.

Consent layers

Layer Applies to Who can grant Default
Individual consent Personal interview, likeness, voice, biography, workshop photos Individual practitioner or participant Restricted until signed
Practitioner consent Teaching scope, workshop access, profile content, compensation terms Practitioner Not public
Community consent Shared practices, local heritage claims, sensitive place context Recognized community steward or partner Restricted
Land / site consent Farm, forest, workshop, home, private access route Land steward, site owner or authorized host Exact location hidden
Archive consent Storage, metadata, transcript, media access tier, future use Participant plus archive steward Restricted
Student consent Participation, conduct, safety, documentation, media rules Student Required before visit

Individual consent does not substitute for community consent when a shared practice, place or vulnerable lineage is affected.

FPIC-aligned workflow

Step Requirement Output
1. Context check Identify sensitivity, affected people, site and possible harms Documentation harm assessment
2. Prior notice Explain purpose, storage, audience, compensation and withdrawal rights before documentation Plain-language consent brief
3. Consent conversation Review questions, media types, archive tiers and restricted options Consent notes
4. Documentation Capture only approved materials; mark restrictions during capture Field record
5. Participant review Share draft profile, captions, clips or transcript excerpts Review decision
6. Steward review Apply Medium / High sensitivity checks Stewardship decision
7. Publication or archive Publish only approved materials; otherwise archive restricted Access record
8. Ongoing review Reconfirm if use, audience or context changes Renewal or restriction

Documentation harm assessment

Before documenting, answer:

  1. Could publication increase unwanted tourism, surveillance or extraction?
  2. Could exact location data expose private homes, fragile sites or restricted land?
  3. Could the material reveal trade knowledge, ritual knowledge or ecological details that should stay private?
  4. Could a student, researcher, brand or institution reuse the material outside the agreed context?
  5. Could the practitioner face social, legal, political or economic harm?
  6. Is there a community-level authority that must review shared knowledge?
  7. Is compensation, attribution and withdrawal clear?

If the answer is uncertain, keep the material restricted.

Access tiers

Tier Description Examples Approval required
Public Approved for website, public profile or public report Short bio, approved photos, general craft description Participant + steward if needed
Community Accessible to named community or partner reviewers Local archive record, review copy Community steward
Practitioner-only Returned to practitioner or kept for their use Full transcript, raw images Practitioner
Internal restricted Studium internal review only field notes, draft profile, consent notes Archive steward
Sealed Stored but not accessed without explicit future approval sensitive interviews, site records Participant or steward re-approval
Delete / withdraw Removed from active archive according to agreement withdrawn media Archive steward records action

Restricted access is the default for all speculative atlas materials and all pre-consent documentation.

Withdrawal rights

Participants may:

  • withdraw unpublished material at any time;
  • restrict previously approved material if circumstances change;
  • request correction of names, captions, locations or context;
  • require exact locations to remain hidden;
  • decline future student access without losing good standing;
  • request deletion when the consent agreement allows it.

For materials already cited, printed or shared externally, Studium must document the limitation and stop future use.

Media rules

Media type Default Required before public use
Audio interview Restricted Transcript excerpt approval
Video interview Restricted Clip approval and context review
Portrait photography Restricted Image selection approval
Workshop process photography Restricted Shot list and trade-knowledge review
Student-generated media Off Student agreement plus practitioner approval
Location data Hidden Explicit site permission
Raw field notes Internal restricted Never public by default

Student documentation conduct

Students may not record, post or publish unless the field protocol explicitly allows it. A student application must include:

  • stated learning intent;
  • agreement to media limits;
  • agreement not to extract or repackage teachings;
  • respect for practitioner and steward instructions;
  • acknowledgement that access can be paused or revoked.

Consent records

Each consent record should include:

  • consent ID;
  • participant or steward role;
  • material types covered;
  • approved uses;
  • denied uses;
  • access tier;
  • compensation or reciprocity terms;
  • review date;
  • withdrawal terms;
  • related archive record IDs.

Do not store signatures, phone numbers, home addresses or sensitive personal details in public CSV files.

Public language constraints

Until consent records exist, public materials may say:

  • Studium is preparing a small Northern Portugal pilot.
  • Practitioner relationships are pending field validation.
  • Documentation will be consent-based and steward-reviewed.

Public materials must not say:

  • named practitioners are participating unless verified;
  • communities endorse Studium unless formally documented;
  • the archive represents a region, lineage or tradition;
  • student access is available before policies are approved.

Studium does not currently operate an apprenticeship program, steward network or institutional partnership structure.

# Studium — Project Overview

Studium is a pre-institutional pilot initiative for connecting learners with craftspeople and land stewards through place-based apprenticeship, documentation and cultural preservation. The current work is a small-scale research prototype focused on Northern Portugal, with governance, consent and archive readiness as the next milestone before field validation.

## Core ideas (one page)

### Operating posture

```
              MASTER
        (holder of knowledge)

STUDENT ---------------- STEWARD
 (seeker)              (guardian of place)
```

The Student · Master · Steward triangle remains a useful internal model, but public positioning should lead with fieldwork, consent, documentation and local review.

### The business

- **Current model:** research prototype and pilot stewardship network
- **Near-term wedge:** one region in Northern Portugal
- **Validation needed:** documented practitioner relationships, field interviews, partner review, compensation framework

### The pilot (year one)

- **Region:** Northern Portugal (Douro, Minho, Serra da Estrela, Trás-os-Montes)
- **Targets:** 3 documented practitioner relationships · 1 tested stewardship review process · 1 measurable apprenticeship model
- **Proof points:** consented documentation, clear participation terms, practitioner trust, local review path

### Operating gate

> Governance, consent and archive controls matter more than software.

Governance, consent and archive controls precede pilot fieldwork. App, marketplace, booking and public map work remain deferred until those controls are tested.

# Community Review Procedure

**Version:** Phase IV draft · 2026-05-17
**Status:** Drafted review procedure before community-facing documentation, archive access changes or public cultural representation

---

## Purpose

This procedure defines how Studium routes documentation, archive permissions and public representation through community review.

This procedure is drafted. It becomes implemented only after named review authorities, review calendars, secure records and audit logs exist.

---

## Review authority

| Review object | Required authority | Required support role | Default without approval |
|---------------|--------------------|-----------------------|--------------------------|
| Shared practice description | Community authority | Consent coordinator | Restricted |
| Community name, place identity or local affiliation | Community authority | Communications owner | Restricted |
| Practitioner profile involving shared heritage | Practitioner plus community authority | Governance steward | Restricted |
| Exact or semi-precise site context | Site authority plus community authority if shared | Archive steward | Region-only or sealed |
| Archive access for community records | Community authority plus archive steward | Archive steward | Community-only or sealed |
| Student access to community setting | Practitioner, site authority and community authority | Governance steward | No access |
| Public cultural representation | Community authority plus communications owner | Governance steward | No public release |

When authority is disputed or unclear, the governance steward assigns sealed access and opens escalation.

---

## Review cadence

| Review type | Frequency | Owner | Output |
|-------------|-----------|-------|--------|
| Pre-documentation authority check | Before outreach or field visit | Consent coordinator | Authority map |
| Archive access review | Quarterly during active pilot | Archive steward | Access tier log |
| Public language review | Before publication and after material changes | Communications owner | Publication approval record |
| Community relationship review | Every 6 months during active relationship | Governance steward | Continue, revise, pause or close decision |
| Withdrawal and dispute review | Within 5 business days for first action | Governance steward | Restriction, repair or escalation decision |
| Annual procedure review | Every 12 months | Advisory reviewer | Procedure revision note |

---

## Approval requirements

Community approval must include:

- reviewer authority basis;
- material or activity under review;
- archive IDs or draft path;
- allowed uses;
- denied uses;
- access tier;
- location precision;
- review expiration or next review date;
- revocation path;
- timestamp;
- person recording the decision.

Verbal approvals require a written decision note with recorder, date, language used and confirmation method.

---

## Review workflow

| Step | Action | Owner | Completion criterion |
|------|--------|-------|----------------------|
| 1. Prepare review packet | Gather draft text, captions, excerpts, access request and harm assessment | Communications owner or archive steward | Packet includes access tier and proposed use |
| 2. Verify authority | Confirm reviewer role and scope | Consent coordinator | Authority map updated |
| 3. Deliver packet | Send or present materials in agreed language and format | Consent coordinator | Delivery timestamp logged |
| 4. Record decision | Capture approve, revise, restrict, seal, withdraw or escalate | Consent coordinator | Decision record complete |
| 5. Apply decision | Update draft, archive tier, public page or storage access | Responsible owner | Affected surfaces changed |
| 6. Audit | Record final state, owner, timestamp and next review date | Archive steward | Audit log entry exists |
| 7. Confirm closeout | Notify reviewer of action taken where appropriate | Consent coordinator | Closeout note logged |

---

## Revision procedure

When a reviewer requests revision:

1. Communications owner or documentation owner pauses publication.
2. Archive steward keeps related material restricted.
3. Requested changes are logged by section, caption, clip, image or metadata field.
4. Revised material returns to the reviewer unless the reviewer waives second review.
5. Approval record identifies the final version.
6. Superseded drafts remain restricted or are deleted according to consent terms.

No revised public material may rely on approval for an earlier version unless the approval record names that version.

---

## Takedown process

| Timeframe | Action | Owner |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Same business day | Acknowledge takedown request and identify affected materials | Communications owner |
| Within 2 business days | Suspend public access while review proceeds | Communications owner plus archive steward |
| Within 5 business days | Remove, revise or restrict public material | Communications owner |
| Within 10 business days | Complete review of related archive access tiers | Archive steward |
| By next review meeting | Record root cause and prevention action | Governance steward |

Takedown records must include public paths, archive IDs, requester authority, action timestamp and residual exposure if external copies exist.

---

## Audit logging

Each review event must record:

- review ID;
- archive IDs or document paths;
- reviewer authority type;
- owner who delivered the packet;
- owner who recorded the decision;
- decision timestamp;
- decision category;
- access tier before and after;
- public surface affected;
- follow-up owner;
- next review date;
- unresolved dispute flag.

Audit logs must use coded identifiers for sensitive people, sites or reasoning when stored in repository files.

---

## Escalation path

| Condition | Immediate action | Escalation owner | Final default |
|-----------|------------------|------------------|---------------|
| Authority cannot be verified | Seal material | Governance steward | No publication |
| Reviewers disagree | Restrict material | Governance steward plus advisory reviewer | Narrowest access tier |
| Reviewer requests removal | Suspend access | Archive steward | Removed or sealed unless reviewer approves another tier |
| Harm allegation appears | Suspend access and log incident | Governance steward | No public use until resolved |
| External obligation conflicts with consent | Stop new use | Governance steward plus legal advisor | Consent restriction controls use |

---

## Completion criteria

A community review cycle is complete only when:

1. authority basis is recorded;
2. review packet is logged;
3. decision is logged with timestamp;
4. access tier matches the decision;
5. public surface, if any, matches approved text and media;
6. revocation path is recorded;
7. next review date exists;
8. unresolved disputes are escalated or remain restricted.

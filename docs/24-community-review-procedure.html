Purpose

This procedure defines how Studium routes documentation, archive permissions and public representation through community review.

This procedure is drafted. It becomes implemented only after named review authorities, review calendars, secure records and audit logs exist.

When authority is disputed or unclear, the governance steward assigns sealed access and opens escalation.

Completion criteria

  1. Authority basis is recorded.
  2. Review packet is logged.
  3. Decision is logged with timestamp.
  4. Access tier matches the decision.
  5. Public surface, if any, matches approved text and media.
  6. Revocation path is recorded.
  7. Next review date exists.
  8. Unresolved disputes are escalated or remain restricted.

Studium does not operate an apprenticeship program, steward network or institutional partnership structure.

The Studium Triangle
Student • Master • Steward
Studium is built on a three-sided ecosystem.
Master — Keeper of Knowledge
Masters carry knowledge through:
decades of practice
lineage or family tradition
cultural memory
land stewardship.
Examples:
Murano glass master
Kyoto indigo dyer
Oaxaca Zapotec weaver
Douro vineyard steward
Masters are living archives, not service providers.
What masters need
respect for their craft
protection of tradition

financial sustainability.
Student — Seeker of Knowledge
Students are not tourists.
They arrive with intention to learn.
Examples
artists
chefs
architects

They come to:
observe

contribute.
Steward — Guardian of Place

Examples:
